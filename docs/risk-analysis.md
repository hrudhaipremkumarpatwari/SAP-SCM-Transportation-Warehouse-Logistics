# Risk Analysis and Contingency Plan

| Risk | Operational Impact | Control / Contingency |
|---|---|---|
| Carrier delay or no-show | Missed departure, delivery delay | backup carrier list, earlier confirmation, escalation threshold |
| Vehicle breakdown | In-transit delay | replacement vehicle, carrier escalation, customer ETA update |
| Low vehicle fill | Higher cost per tonne | consolidate compatible loads, review shipment frequency |
| Dock congestion | Loading delays | slot appointments, wave-to-door alignment, overflow staging |
| Picking delay | Truck waiting time | priority waves, labor reallocation, exception monitoring |
| Inventory mismatch | Failed picking / short shipment | cycle-count checks, stock reconciliation, blocked-bin review |
| Incorrect route or master data | Wrong plan or timing | master-data validation and change approval |
| Integration failure | TM/EWM/ERP status mismatch | interface monitoring, retry procedure, manual fallback |
| Peak occupancy | Congestion and safety risk | temporary overflow area, tighter replenishment, slotting review |
| Demand spike | Extra transport and warehouse load | flexible carrier capacity, overtime, priority-based allocation |

## Governance

Transportation planners own routing and freight-planning accuracy, warehouse supervisors own execution readiness, inventory teams own stock accuracy, and integration/support teams own interface monitoring. Repeated exceptions should be reviewed weekly and converted into parameter or master-data corrections where appropriate.
